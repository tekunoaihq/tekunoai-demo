Flagship

AI Agent Universe

A two way AI agent universe where a user's buyer agent and a seller agent discover, compare, negotiate and complete a purchase within the user's budget and approval rules. The buyer agent never exceeds the user's max budget and the seller agent never goes below its floor price.

Verified demo: headphones listed at $120 (floor $80), buyer target $70, budget $100 Result: DEAL closed at $94 after 2 rounds, inside budget and above floor. Second run: premium watch listed at $500 (floor $450) vs buyer budget $300 Result: correct WALK AWAY, no deal forced. Tests: 5 passing

AISDLC Management System

AI agents coordinate the software development lifecycle: requirements, design, development, testing, review, deployment and monitoring, with quality gates that block a task when acceptance criteria or required checks are missing.

Verified demo: 3 tasks completed through all 7 SDLC phases, 100 percent completion. A blocked example correctly stops at the requirements gate for missing acceptance criteria. Tests: 11 passing

AI Powered Event Management

AI assisted event planning: schedule generation, vendor and attendee management, and live budget tracking that warns before spend crosses the event budget.
